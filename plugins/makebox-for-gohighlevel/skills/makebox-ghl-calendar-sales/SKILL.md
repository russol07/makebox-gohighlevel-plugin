description: Review GoHighLevel calendars, appointments, sales opportunities and pipelines in an authorized location.
---

For bookings identify the calendar, contact, location timezone, service and relevant availability before suggesting or creating a slot. A returned time is not a confirmed booking. For sales, identify the pipeline, stage and opportunity; treat page counts and total counts separately. Use current registered tool schemas and provider results. Only make a supported change within the user's requested scope; report a disabled write, conflict or missing scope. Read back successful changes where possible.
