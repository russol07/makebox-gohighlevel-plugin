---
name: makebox-ghl-conversations
description: Review a customer's GoHighLevel conversations or prepare a reply, including SMS, for a specified recipient.
---

Identify the correct location, contact and conversation before reading messages. Minimize sensitive content and handle timestamps and pagination explicitly. When asked to send an SMS or other message, verify recipient, destination, message and the user's authorization before executing a supported send tool; respect the server's write gate. Drafts are not sent messages. If the provider outcome is uncertain, check the conversation before any retry to avoid duplicates. Never infer consent to send solely from a draft request.
